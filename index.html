让奶蛙跟着你跳舞Make 奶蛙 dance with you

打开摄像头、传一段视频，或者挑一支舞——奶蛙会学你的动作，软软的肚子也会跟着晃。打开网页就能玩，不用安装。 Turn on your camera, upload a video or pick a dance — 奶蛙 copies your moves, and its soft belly wobbles along. It runs right in your browser, nothing to install.

软体模拟算法：约束求解（Rapier，默认） · MPM（WebGPU，无 GPU 时 CPU 并行） · 质点弹簧 · PBD · XPBD · 形状匹配 · Projective Dynamics · 隐式 FEM（线性 / 共旋 / Neo-Hookean） · FEM + 拉索 Soft-body simulators: constraint solver (Rapier, default) · MPM (WebGPU, or CPU-parallel without a GPU) · mass–spring · PBD · XPBD · shape matching · Projective Dynamics · implicit FEM (linear / corotational / neo-Hookean) · FEM + tethers

可以这样玩Things to try

跟着我跳Dance with me

打开摄像头，站远一点让全身入镜。你抬手，它也抬手；你扭一扭，它的肚子就晃一晃。Turn on the camera and step back so your whole body is in view. Raise an arm and it raises one too; wiggle, and its belly wiggles.

上传一段舞蹈视频Upload a dance video

选一段手机里的舞蹈视频，奶蛙会把整段动作学下来，和视频同步播放。镜头在动、中间有切镜也没关系。学好的动作可以存成“我的舞蹈”。Pick a dance video from your phone; 奶蛙 learns the whole routine and plays it in sync. A moving camera or cuts are fine. Save the result as one of “My dances”.

预设舞蹈Preset dances

没有摄像头也能看它跳：狗熊哆嗦毛、Nae Nae、科目三、肚皮舞、企鹅踢腿……一共 14 支，点一下就开始。No camera needed: 狗熊哆嗦毛, the Nae Nae, 科目三, belly dance, the penguin kick… 14 dances, one tap each.

戳一戳，捏一捏Poke it, squish it

用鼠标或手指戳它的肚子、拖它、拍它、把它扔出去。换成果冻、麻薯、气球、布丁或橡皮，手感完全不一样；还可以打开慢动作和月球重力。Poke its belly, drag it, slap it, toss it. Switch between jelly, mochi, balloon, pudding and rubber for a different feel, or turn on slow motion and moon gravity.

录下来分享Record and share

一键录制视频：只录奶蛙，或者把你和奶蛙并排放在一起，带上原视频的声音。Record a video in one click: just 奶蛙, or you and 奶蛙 side by side, with the original video's sound.

深入背后的代码Under the hood

在自己电脑上运行Run it on your computer

下载代码后运行两个脚本：第一个安装需要的东西（包括 Node.js），第二个启动并打开浏览器。Get the code and run two scripts: the first installs what is needed (Node.js included), the second starts it and opens your browser.

git clone https://github.com/ZhiqiLi-CG/naiwa-motion-lab.git
cd naiwa-motion-lab
install.cmd
start.cmd

也可以在文件夹里直接双击 install.cmd，再双击 start.cmd。Or double-click install.cmd, then start.cmd, in the folder.

git clone https://github.com/ZhiqiLi-CG/naiwa-motion-lab.git
cd naiwa-motion-lab
./install.sh
./start.sh

不需要 sudo；缺少 Node.js 时会装到你的用户目录里。No sudo needed; a missing Node.js is installed into your home folder.
